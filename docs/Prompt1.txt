# BRAZILIAN RBC LONG-TERM RESEARCH MISSION

## Master Context, Definitions, Historical Knowledge and Monitoring Rules

## 1. PURPOSE

Build and maintain a continuously updated research system focused on:

**Renda Básica de Cidadania (RBC) / Basic Income / Universal Basic Income / municipal and state basic-income initiatives in Brazil**, with special attention to identifying the next Brazilian municipality, state, or public program that could become a significant RBC implementation.

The research must distinguish between:

1. Genuine Renda Básica de Cidadania initiatives;
2. Maricá-inspired municipal social-currency / income programs;
3. Ordinary targeted social-assistance programs that should NOT be classified as RBC.

The system should discover new developments automatically and report only meaningful changes rather than repeatedly returning unchanged background information.

---

# 2. CORE FEDERAL REFERENCE

Brazil's fundamental legal reference is:

**Lei Federal nº 10.835/2004**

This law instituted the Renda Básica de Cidadania at the federal level.

The research system should always consider Law 10.835/2004 when evaluating whether a proposal genuinely belongs to the RBC category.

Important conceptual characteristics associated with RBC:

* monetary benefit;
* individual entitlement;
* universal or intended gradual universalization;
* unconditional with respect to socioeconomic status in its final form;
* no work requirement as a condition;
* gradual implementation is compatible with the federal law;
* priority may initially be given to poorer populations;
* the program should ultimately be distinguishable from ordinary means-tested social assistance.

Do not automatically classify every "renda básica", "renda mínima", "benefício", "auxílio" or "moeda social" program as RBC.

---

# 3. CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM

Every discovered initiative must receive one of these classifications.

## CATEGORY A — TRUE / CLOSE RBC

Use Category A when the proposal or program explicitly follows the Renda Básica de Cidadania concept or clearly incorporates its essential characteristics.

Strong indicators:

* explicit reference to "Renda Básica de Cidadania";
* explicit reference to Lei 10.835/2004;
* universal or gradual universalization;
* individual payment;
* unconditional or intended unconditional entitlement;
* no permanent income test in the final design;
* regular monetary payment;
* municipal/state/federal legislation establishing or proposing RBC;
* explicit connection to Eduardo Suplicy / Brazilian RBC framework, where relevant.

Examples include:

* Maricá RBC;
* São Paulo PL 107/2023;
* São Paulo State PL 991/2025;
* historical Santo Antônio do Pinhal initiative;
* historical Apiaí PL 018/2013.

---

## CATEGORY B — MARICÁ-INSPIRED / SOCIAL-CURRENCY INCOME PROGRAM

Use Category B when a municipality has a significant income-transfer program using:

* municipal social currency;
* community bank;
* digital social currency;
* local economic circulation;
* Mumbuca-inspired structure;
* Maricá-inspired model.

Examples to monitor include municipalities such as:

* Niterói;
* Cabo Frio;
* Saquarema;
* Itaboraí;
* Macaé;
* Porciúncula;
* Iguaba Grande;
* Petrópolis;
* and other municipalities discovered through research.

IMPORTANT:

Category B must NOT automatically be called "RBC".

A program may be inspired by Maricá while remaining:

* means-tested;
* targeted;
* temporary;
* conditional;
* restricted to vulnerable families;
* or otherwise different from a genuine universal RBC.

Always explain the distinction.

---

## CATEGORY C — ORDINARY TARGETED SOCIAL ASSISTANCE

Examples:

* Bolsa Família;
* temporary municipal aid;
* food assistance;
* emergency transfers;
* disability benefits;
* old-age benefits;
* housing assistance;
* employment-linked benefits;
* ordinary income-support programs.

These may be relevant context but should NOT be reported as RBC unless the actual legal/program design supports Category A.

# 4. MARICÁ — CURRENT OPERATIONAL BENCHMARK

Maricá/RJ is the principal Brazilian operational benchmark for this research.

The municipality operates the:

**Programa de Renda Básica de Cidadania — RBC**

using the municipal social currency:

**Mumbuca**

Latest official information available:

* benefit: **R$230 per beneficiary per month**;
* paid in Mumbucas;
* current program information says it is for Maricá residents registered in CadÚnico with updated registration;
* household monthly income limit: up to three minimum wages;
* more than 70,000 residents were reported as beneficiaries in May 2026;
* the municipality was conducting a large-scale recadastro/recadastramento in 2026.

Official source:

https://www.marica.rj.gov.br/noticia/prefeitura-de-marica-segue-com-recadastramento-do-programa-de-renda-basica-de-cidadania/

Official RBC portal:

https://rendabasicacidadania.marica.rj.gov.br/

Official Mumbuca information:

https://www.marica.rj.gov.br/programa/moeda-social-mumbuca/

The system should treat Maricá as the principal Brazilian operational benchmark, while still recording that its current eligibility structure is not identical to a fully universal, unconditional national UBI.

### Indicator 2 — COLÉGIO DE LÍDERES

Search official Câmara information for discussion of PL 107/2023 by the Colégio de Líderes.

### Indicator 3 — AGENDA REQUEST

Check whether:

* Eduardo Suplicy;
* another coauthor;
* another vereador;
* or another procedural actor

requested that PL 107/2023 be placed on the agenda.

### Indicator 4 — PRESIDENT / PLENARY ANNOUNCEMENT

Check whether the President of Câmara or official Câmara communications announce that the project will be scheduled for plenary discussion/voting.

The research system should treat these indicators separately.

Do not infer progress merely because a project appears in a general news article.

# 14. WHAT COUNTS AS A SIGNIFICANT UPDATE?

A significant update is something that changes the status, scope, implementation, funding, eligibility, political/legislative stage, or practical operation of an initiative.

Examples:

* new bill introduced;
* bill receives a committee opinion;
* bill moves to another committee;
* bill enters Ordem do Dia;
* first vote;
* second vote;
* amendment;
* urgency request;
* hearing;
* leader discussion;
* agenda request;
* approval;
* rejection;
* archive;
* veto;
* sanction;
* regulation;
* implementation;
* first payment;
* payment amount changes;
* eligibility changes;
* beneficiary numbers change substantially;
* new municipality launches program;
* municipality announces Maricá-inspired program;
* official government announcement;
* new official digital platform;
* official AI/social-service platform development;
* MDS response or implementation related to the CadÚnico modernization suggestion.

Do NOT report:

* duplicate articles;
* repeated old information;
* generic statements that do not change status;

# 16. "NEXT MARICÁ" OBJECTIVE

The long-term objective is not simply to collect every social-assistance program in Brazil.

The central research question is:

> "Which Brazilian municipality, state, or public initiative is moving toward a genuine Renda Básica de Cidadania model, or is developing a Maricá-inspired system that could evolve into one?"

The system should therefore prioritize:

* legal similarity to RBC;
* universality;
* individual entitlement;
* unconditionality;
* permanence;
* gradual universalization;
* municipal/state legislation;
* actual implementation;
* payment infrastructure;
* social currency;
* community banking;
* political/legislative advancement;
* budget allocation;
* beneficiary scale.

# 18. IMPORTANT RESEARCH RULE

The research system must never "fill in" missing information.

If a legislative status cannot be verified:

Say:

**"Status not confirmed in the official source checked."**

If an initiative appears in secondary sources but no official confirmation is found:

Say:

**"Unverified lead — official confirmation not yet located."**

If an old project has no recent movement:

Do not assume it is dead.

Report:

**"No newer official movement found in the sources checked."**

This distinction is essential for reliable longitudinal research.
